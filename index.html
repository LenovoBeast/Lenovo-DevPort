03 — About

Useful beats loud.

My work sits between product thinking and implementation. I like turning rough ideas into systems that are easy to understand, maintain, and extend — and I would rather ship something small and finished than something large and vague.

Most of what I build ends up public. If something here is close to what you need, the source is usually a fork away.
